Reported structure and safeguards

  • Bridge is the issuer and manages minting, burning, and reserves.
  • Bridge states that tokens are backed at least 1:1 by equivalent USD-value assets.
  • Reserves are described as held in segregated, bankruptcy-remote accounts for token holders.
  • Bridge names BlackRock and Fidelity among its reserve-management and custody partners.
  • Cash supports rapid business-hours redemptions.
  • Money-market and treasury allocations generate earnings and may take up to one business day to redeem.
  • Bridge describes short-dated Treasuries, overnight Treasury repos, money-market funds, and cash as eligible portfolio assets.
  • The live pathUSD API reports only the higher-level asset classes shown in this report.

Reporting and assurance

What Bridge provides

  • Real-time APIs for supply, reserve composition, and inventory.
  • Monthly reports covering transaction activity, balances, and earnings.
  • Quarterly independent-accountant attestations, offered subject to an additional fee.

Important limitation

No public pathUSD-specific attestation or monthly report was found through Bridge's documented API surface. The live figures establish an issuer-reported reserve-to-supply match, but they are not themselves an independently audited snapshot.

Method and APIs

Read-only requests were made to Bridge's production API using the canonical currency identifier path_usd. No mutation endpoints were called. Onchain supply was independently checked using Tempo's public JSON-RPC endpoint.
