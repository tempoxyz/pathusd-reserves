Structure and safeguards

As reported by Bridge

Issuance and custody

  • Bridge is the issuer and manages minting, burning, and reserves.
  • Tokens are described as backed at least 1:1 by equivalent USD-value assets.
  • Reserves are described as segregated and bankruptcy remote for token holders.
  • Bridge names BlackRock and Fidelity among reserve-management and custody partners.

Liquidity profile

  • Cash supports rapid business-hours redemptions.
  • Money-market and treasury assets may take up to one business day to redeem.
  • Eligible assets can include short-dated Treasuries, repos, money-market funds, and cash.
  • The live pathUSD API reports only the higher-level asset classes shown above.

Reporting and assurance

Disclosure coverage

What Bridge provides

  • Real-time APIs for supply, reserve composition, and inventory.
  • Monthly reports covering activity, balances, and earnings.
  • Quarterly independent-accountant attestations, subject to an additional fee.
i No public pathUSD-specific attestation or monthly report was found through Bridge's documented API surface. These live figures are issuer-reported API data, not an independently audited snapshot.

Method and APIs

Read-only requests

The page refreshes through a same-origin Vercel Function, keeping Bridge credentials server-side. Its response is cached at the edge for five minutes and may be served stale for up to one hour while it revalidates. No mutation endpoints are called. Supply is independently checked with Tempo's public JSON-RPC endpoint.
