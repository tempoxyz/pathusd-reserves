Sources and methodology

This dashboard is informational, not an independent reserve attestation. Reserve responses cache for five minutes and may be served stale for up to an hour while revalidating. Daily history caches hourly.

Bridge reports pathUSD reserves, supply and orchestration inventory. Inventory is not added to reserve assets. Reserve coverage compares reported USD assets with token supply; the onchain check uses totalSupply() at the displayed Tempo block.
