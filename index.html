Legal issuer and redemption

Generic Bridge Open Issuance documentation

Documented platform structure

Bridge's minting and burning documentation says Bridge is issuer and handles minting and burning; its Open Issuance overview says Bridge manages reserves. Bridge's end-user FAQ describes reserves as segregated and bankruptcy remote. These are Bridge's generic platform statements, not pathUSD-specific controlling terms.

Documented mint and redemption flow

Bridge says a user deposit is detected, allocated to reserve accounts, and followed by minting equivalent tokens. On redemption, Bridge says it detects incoming tokens, burns them, releases equivalent fiat value, then sends fiat to a bank account or crypto to a specified address. Bridge's design documentation describes Open Loop assets as public and permissionless; this does not establish pathUSD's legal redemption eligibility.

Methodology and freshness

Reserve data is read through a credential-protecting same-origin function, cached for five minutes, and may be served stale for up to one hour during revalidation. Daily history is warehouse-derived and cached hourly. Supply is checked read-only against Tempo at one reported block; all other live values remain issuer-reported.
